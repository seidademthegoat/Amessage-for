Hello, My Life

From Seid Adem

Hello Nadiya, hello my life.

You came into my life like a random girl, but somehow you found your way into my heart. Since then, I have had so many feelings that sometimes I cannot find the words to explain them.

Do you know why I prefer writing this message instead of talking to you face to face? Because I become fearful in front of you. Even when I text you, my heart races so hard. And when I see you face to face, I forget everything I had planned to say.

I do not know why this happens. Maybe it is because you mean so much to me. I hope you can understand.

Look at how the days have been going. Today is Friday, and we are seeing each other, but we are not saying much. I keep asking myself: is this really how things are supposed to be?

I am also a human being, Nadiya. I have feelings too. I care about you, but I do not know how to get closer when I am unsure about what you feel.

I called you two days ago, and we talked for a short time. I know you have your own life, your own schedule, and your own things to do. But I have been carrying these questions in my heart.

I am not writing this to make you feel guilty. I am writing because I want us to understand each other honestly.

Nadiya, do you have feelings for me? Do you really love me, or have your feelings changed?

I want to understand where I stand in your life. I want to know whether you still want us to have a relationship, or whether you would prefer that we remain friends.

Whatever your answer is, I would rather hear the truth than continue wondering.

I have not forgotten our conversations. I have not forgotten the sweet words, the secrets we shared, and the promises we talked about.

I still remember when you asked me about the villain and the hero. I remember the summer when we spent so much time being happy together. Those were some of the happiest memories I have had.

I am so thankful that you trusted me. That trust meant a lot to me, and I will always appreciate the memories we made.

Then one day, you disappeared. You came back, and you had a schedule. I respected that. When the schedule did not work out, I stayed quiet because I did not want to pressure you.

Then you disappeared again. You came back on my birthday, and I was happy to hear from you. But after that, things became difficult for me to understand.

When you sent me this message:

"Okay, this is serious. I care, but I don't want to continue this relationship in the way we've been doing. I need to keep my boundaries and focus on what is right. Please respect that."

I respected what you said, but I did not fully understand what you meant by "the way we've been doing."

I had been waiting to hear from you. I was checking for your messages and hoping we could talk. So when I received that message, I felt confused and hurt.

When I asked you at school, you said things were normal. But for me, they did not feel normal. I wanted to understand what had happened.

Nadiya, please help me understand. If I did something wrong, tell me. If I crossed a boundary, tell me. If there is something I need to learn, I want to understand it.

I am not saying I am perfect. I am only asking for an honest conversation. I do not want to blame you. I want to know what is true.

I called because I wanted us to talk. When we did not talk much, I started feeling like I was being ignored. I know that may not have been your intention, but that is how it felt to me.

I have not forgotten what I promised you. I have not forgotten what we shared. But I also understand that you have the right to your own feelings, your own boundaries, and your own choices.

I do not want you to feel forced to love me. I do not want you to answer because you feel guilty. I want you to be honest with me, even if the answer is difficult for me.

All I ask is that you read this carefully and tell me what you truly feel.

I would rather have an honest answer than keep guessing what is in your heart.

Thank you for trusting me. Thank you for the memories. Thank you for the time we shared.

Whatever happens, I hope we can treat each other with kindness and respect. And also don't wait until i call you because it is your answer now

Seid Adem The fearful one who finally found the words.
